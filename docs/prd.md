# KASHI — A City Beyond Time
## Product Requirements Document (PRD)

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Implementation Specification / Master Product Specification  
**Product Type:** Immersive Digital Cultural Experience / Interactive Portfolio Website  
**Primary Subject:** Kashi / Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh, India  
**Primary Experience:** Desktop-first cinematic web experience with responsive mobile adaptation

---

# 1. Product Overview

## 1.1 Product Name

**KASHI — A City Beyond Time**

## 1.2 Product Summary

KASHI — A City Beyond Time is an immersive digital experience created to represent the cultural, spiritual, historical, architectural, culinary, visual, and emotional identity of Kashi (Varanasi).

The product is **not a conventional tourism website**.

It should feel like entering a digital interpretation of Kashi: a cinematic journey in which users discover the city through carefully designed chapters, photographs, typography, motion, 3D interactions, historical storytelling, editorial poster compositions, and atmospheric transitions.

The experience should combine:

- Cinematic photography
- Editorial typography
- Interactive storytelling
- Scroll-driven motion
- 3D objects
- Subtle environmental animation
- Cinematic page transitions
- Historical and mythological storytelling
- Retro editorial travel-poster aesthetics
- Interactive visual compositions
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Responsive interaction design

The central product idea is:

> **Kashi is not simply a place to visit. It is a place to experience.**

## 1.3 Product Positioning

The website should feel closer to:

**an interactive digital exhibition + cinematic documentary + editorial travel experience**

than:

**a conventional tourism portal.**

The product should communicate that Kashi is simultaneously:

- Ancient and contemporary
- Spiritual and ordinary
- Chaotic and peaceful
- Historical and constantly evolving
- Sacred and deeply human
- Architectural and experiential

## 1.4 Core Product Promise

The product should allow a user who may know little about Kashi to leave with:

1. A stronger emotional connection to the city.
2. A visual understanding of its most important places and experiences.
3. A basic understanding of its historical and spiritual significance.
4. A desire to experience Kashi physically.

# 2. Problem Statement

## 2.1 The Problem

Most destination websites use a conventional information architecture:

> Hero → About → Places → Food → Gallery → Contact

Although this works for functional travel planning, it does not adequately communicate the emotional and cultural complexity of Kashi.

A conventional website risks reducing Kashi to:

- photographs
- location names
- short descriptions
- tourist attractions
- generic calls to action

This approach communicates **information about Kashi**, but not necessarily **the feeling of Kashi**.

## 2.2 Core Product Problem

**How can a digital experience communicate the atmosphere, history, spirituality, visual identity, cultural depth, and emotional character of Kashi rather than simply providing information about it?**

## 2.3 Product Opportunity

Kashi is particularly suited to immersive storytelling because its identity is layered across:

- the Ganga
- the ghats
- temples
- mythology
- documented history
- rituals
- architecture
- food
- narrow streets
- evening atmosphere
- contemporary public spaces
- everyday human activity

The website can use modern web technology to reveal these layers progressively rather than presenting them as a flat list.

## 2.4 Problem for the User

A visitor may ask:

- What actually makes Kashi different?
- Why are the ghats important?
- Why is Shiva so deeply associated with Kashi?
- What does the city look and feel like beyond famous photographs?
- What places define the city?
- What does Kashi taste like?
- What is the relationship between its ancient identity and present-day life?

The product should answer these questions through **experience + information**, rather than information alone.

# 3. Product Vision

Create a highly immersive digital interpretation of Kashi that makes users feel as though they are progressively entering, understanding, exploring, and finally reflecting on the city.

The product should demonstrate that modern web technology can create a meaningful cultural experience rather than merely decorative animation.

The website should leave the user with the feeling:

> **“I did not just browse Kashi. I experienced a version of it.”**

# 8. Core Experience Principles

## Principle 1 — Cinematic, Not Cluttered

Every element should have visual breathing room.

Avoid:

- Excessive cards
- Excessive badges
- Dense paragraphs
- Too many buttons
- Generic dashboard-like UI
- Unnecessary UI chrome

---

## Principle 2 — Motion Must Have Purpose

Animation should communicate:

- Transition
- Depth
- Hierarchy
- Discovery
- Continuity
- Emotional pacing

Do not animate an element simply because it can be animated.

---

## Principle 3 — Photography Remains Authentic

Finalized photographs are important product assets.

They must not be replaced or unnecessarily redesigned.

---

## Principle 4 — Kashi Is the Protagonist

The website should never feel like:

> “Look at how impressive our animations are.”

It should feel like:

> “Look at Kashi.”

Technology exists to amplify the subject.

---

## Principle 5 — Controlled Visual Complexity

The experience can be sophisticated without becoming confusing.

At all times, users should understand:

- Where they are
- What they are viewing
- How to continue
- How to go back

---

## Principle 6 — Progressive Discovery

Do not expose every piece of information immediately.

The experience should reveal information progressively through:

- transitions
- scroll
- hover
- click
- visual hierarchy
- chapter progression

---

## Principle 7 — Respect the Subject

Kashi should not be reduced to a stereotypical visual of temples and spirituality.

The product should communicate the coexistence of:

- sacred and ordinary
- ancient and modern
- stillness and chaos
- ritual and daily life

# 11. Core Features

# CF-01 — The Infinite Door

## Purpose

Create an emotional threshold between the user's normal browsing environment and the Kashi experience.

## Visual Requirements

- Near-total darkness
- Monumental minimal doorway/portal
- White/golden light emerging through the opening
- Volumetric light rays
- Realistic 3D human silhouette
- Figure walking toward the doorway
- Quote integrated into the composition
- No temple architecture in the doorway scene

## Interaction

The figure approaches the doorway.

When the figure crosses the threshold:

**Full-screen white exposure transition**

The transition reveals the landing page.

## Timing Requirement

Opening duration:

**≤ 12 seconds**

## UX Requirement

The experience must not trap the user.

A user must be able to continue without unnecessary interaction.

---

# CF-02 — KASHI Landing

## Title

**KASHI — A City Beyond Time**

## Purpose

Establish Kashi as a complete city rather than focusing exclusively on spirituality.

## Visual Language

- Morning atmosphere

- Large typography
- Restrained interface

## Requirements

The landing page must provide access to the major chapters.

Navigation should feel integrated into the visual composition rather than resembling a conventional application navbar.

# CF-03 — Steps to Eternity

## Purpose

Represent the ghats as living spaces rather than simply listing attractions.

## Final Ghat List

1. Assi Ghat
2. Dashashwamedh Ghat
3. Manikarnika Ghat
4. Kedar Ghat
5. Harishchandra Ghat
6. Guleria Ghat
7. Chet Singh Ghat
8. Namo Ghat

## Core Visual Treatment

Each ghat receives:

- One finalized hero photograph
- Ghat name
- Supporting description
- Contextual information
- Subtle environmental animation

## Living Photograph

The photograph should behave subtly like a moving image without becoming an actual video.

Possible effects:

- Slow camera push
- Parallax
- Subtle depth separation
- Water movement
- Atmospheric movement
- Light movement
- Film grain
- Micro-motion

The photograph remains the primary visual.

## Typography Behavior

Initial state:

**Ghat name centered**

During scroll/interaction:

**Ghat name transitions toward the left corner**

This creates the feeling of entering each individual ghat chapter.

## Asset Preservation Rule

The finalized ghat photographs are product assets.

**Do not replace, redesign or substitute them unless explicitly instructed.**

# CF-04 — Story of Kashi

## Purpose

Provide a concise narrative explaining the historical and mythological significance of Kashi.

## Content Requirements

The section must not become a long historical article.

It should function as a short cinematic experience.

## Suggested Four-Chapter Structure

### Chapter 1 — Origins

Introduce the ancient identity of Kashi.

### Chapter 2 — Shiva and Kashi

Explain the cultural and mythological association between Shiva and Kashi.

### Chapter 3 — Ganga and the City

Introduce the relationship between the Ganga and the city's identity.

### Chapter 4 — Kashi Through Time

Connect ancient identity with the continuously evolving city.

## Content Integrity

Historical facts and religious traditions must be distinguishable.

Mythological traditions should not be presented as verified historical facts.

## Emotional Statement

The established Hindi line may be used as a key emotional moment:

> “जहाँ कण-कण में शिव का वास है, और हर घाट पर महादेव का अहसास है, वो हमारी नगरी काशी है।”

- Main visual
- Title
- Tagline
- Description
- Relevant supporting information

Do not turn every poster into a completely separate animation showcase.

# 19. Accessibility

The product must support:

- Keyboard navigation
- Visible focus states
- Semantic HTML
- Meaningful image alt text
- Sufficient contrast
- Reduced-motion preference
- Readable typography
- Accessible interactive controls

Animations must never be the only way information is communicated.

## Reduced Motion

When the user has enabled reduced motion:

- Replace major parallax with static positioning
- Reduce camera movement
- Reduce transition intensity
- Disable unnecessary looping motion
- Preserve the content hierarchy

---

# 20. Performance Requirements

Performance is a first-class product requirement.

## 20.1 Core Principles

- Optimize images
- Lazy-load expensive assets
- Lazy-load 3D
- Avoid blocking JavaScript
- Keep animation loops efficient
- Avoid unnecessary DOM complexity
- Minimize initial bundle cost

## 20.2 Loading Strategy

Do not load the entire experience at once.

Only load expensive assets when they are needed.

## 20.3 Performance Budget Philosophy

Every expensive visual feature must justify its cost.

If a feature significantly reduces performance without materially improving the experience, it should be simplified or removed.

# 37. Final Product Statement

KASHI — A City Beyond Time is not intended to document every fact about Varanasi.

It is intended to capture something more difficult:

**the feeling of encountering Kashi.**

The product should take the user from:

**darkness → arrival → discovery → history → spirituality → exploration → taste → reflection**

and leave them with the sense that the city existed long before the screen and will continue long after the experience ends.

> **Kashi is not a destination inside the website.**
>
> **The website is a doorway into Kashi.**
